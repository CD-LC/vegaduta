# Northwind Bikes – Returns and Refunds Policy

Northwind Bikes is a fictional bicycle shop used for product demonstrations.

## Return window
- Bikes and e-bikes can be returned within **30 days** of delivery.
- Accessories (helmets, lights, locks, pumps) can be returned within **60 days**.
- The item must be unused, with all tags, manuals and the original packaging.
- A proof of purchase (order number or receipt) is required.

## Refunds
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within **5 to 7 business days** after we receive the item.
- Shipping fees are refunded only when the return is due to our error (wrong or damaged item).
- Refunds above **USD 500** need a manager's approval before they are issued.

## Exchanges
- Size exchanges on bikes are free within the 30-day window; we ship the new size once the original is scanned in by the carrier.

## How to start a return
1. Reply to your order e-mail or message us on WhatsApp with your order number.
2. We send a prepaid return label within one business day.
3. Drop the parcel at any carrier point.

## Items we cannot take back
- Custom-built bikes and personalised frames.
- Nutrition, tubes and tyres once the seal is broken.
