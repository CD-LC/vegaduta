# Northwind Bikes – Shipping, Assembly and Stores

Northwind Bikes is a fictional bicycle shop used for product demonstrations.

## Shipping
- Free shipping on orders above USD 99 within the country; USD 15 otherwise.
- Bikes ship 95% assembled: attach the front wheel, pedals and handlebar (tools included), or book free assembly at a store.
- Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days; e-bikes 5 to 8 business days because the battery ships as dangerous goods.

## Stores
- Bengaluru – Indiranagar, 100 Feet Road: Mon–Sat 10:00–20:00, Sun 11:00–18:00.
- Mumbai – Bandra West, Hill Road: Mon–Sat 10:30–20:30, closed Sunday.
- Pune – Koregaon Park: Tue–Sun 10:00–19:00, closed Monday.

## Service
- A free first service is included within 3 months of purchase.
- Book a slot at any store; a standard service takes about 90 minutes.
