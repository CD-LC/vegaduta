# Northwind Bikes – Warranty

Northwind Bikes is a fictional bicycle shop used for product demonstrations.

- **Frames**: lifetime warranty for the original owner against manufacturing defects.
- **E-bike batteries**: 2 years or 500 full charge cycles, whichever comes first. Capacity below 70% inside that period is covered.
- **Motors and controllers**: 2 years.
- **Components** (drivetrain, brakes, wheels): 1 year.
- **Wear parts** (tyres, brake pads, chains, cables) are not covered.

A warranty claim needs the frame serial number (under the bottom bracket), photos of the issue and the original order number. Claims are answered within 3 business days.
