Section 1. Orchard irrigation.
The orchard irrigation controller codename for the north terraces is {{TOKEN}}.
Controllers are inspected every fourteen days by the field crew, and every
inspection is logged in the maintenance register before the next watering cycle.

Section 2. Cold storage.
Cold storage rooms hold produce between two and four degrees Celsius. Door
seals are replaced each spring. Temperature alarms page the duty engineer.

Section 3. Visitor policy.
Visitors must sign in at the gatehouse and wear a high-visibility vest on the
terraces. Drones are not permitted over the packing hall without written approval.
