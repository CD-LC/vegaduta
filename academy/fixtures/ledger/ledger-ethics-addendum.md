# The Ledger — Ethics Addendum

## Sourcing tiers

- **Tier A (publish freely):** on-record officials, published filings,
  court documents, our own measurements.
- **Tier B (one corroboration required):** anonymous desk sources,
  leaked-but-verifiable documents, third-party datasets.
- **Tier C (Editor sign-off only):** single-source tips, commercial
  press releases, anything touching a named private individual.

## Hard rules

1. No headline may assert a number we cannot reproduce from the source
   table within 60 seconds.
2. Corrections publish at the same prominence as the original claim,
   within one edition.
3. The 06:00 embargo is absolute — drafts may exist, nothing publishes.
4. "Guaranteed", "risk-free" and "record-breaking" never appear in copy
   unless quoting a source verbatim, attributed.
5. Photos of private individuals require consent on file; otherwise
   illustrate with context shots only.

## The reader's test

Before any story publishes: would the subject, reading it over coffee,
call it fair? If the answer is "probably not", it goes back to the desk.
