# The Ledger — House Style Guide

The Ledger is a daily AI newspaper for Bangalore readers. Every story follows these rules.

## Tone
- Precise, unsensational, economical. One wry aside per page, never per paragraph.
- Short sentences. Active voice. No exclamation marks outside quoted speech.
- Numbers carry the story: lead with the figure, then the context.

## Embargo
- Nothing publishes before 06:00 IST. Drafts may be prepared overnight; the
  front page is locked at 05:30 IST for fact-check sign-off.

## Sourcing
- Every claim cites a named source or an archived document. "Sources say" is
  not a citation — name the desk, agency or dataset.
- Two independent sources for any figure that moves markets or names a person.
- Unverifiable claims are held for human sign-off, never softened into print.

## Local conventions
- Datelines use neighbourhood names (Indiranagar, Whitefield), not PIN codes.
- Currency in ₹ lakh/crore; convert foreign amounts at the day's RBI rate.
- Metro, BMTC and weather stories run in the City desk, never above the fold
  unless they break a record.
