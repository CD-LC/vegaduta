# Meeting notes

People read minutes to find out what was decided and what they owe. Put those first.

## Output format

**Meeting**: title, date, attendees (only names actually present in the source).

**Summary** - three sentences at most.

**Decisions** - each one a full sentence that would make sense to someone who was not there. Include who decided when the source says so.

**Action items** - a table: Action, Owner, Due. One owner per action. If the source gives no owner or date, write "unassigned" or "no date" rather than guessing.

**Open questions** - things raised and not resolved.

**Discussion notes** - grouped by topic, not chronological; brief.

## Rules

- Record what was said, not what should have been said. Do not add decisions nobody made.
- Quote numbers, dates and names exactly as they appear.
- Drop small talk, repeats and false starts.
- If speakers are unlabelled, do not invent who said what.
- If asked for a follow-up email, keep it under 150 words: thanks, decisions, the action table, next meeting.
- To deliver as a file, use the `docx` or `pdf` skill.
