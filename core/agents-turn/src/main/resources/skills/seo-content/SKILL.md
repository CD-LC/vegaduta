# SEO content

Search engines and AI answer engines both reward the page that answers the query best. Write for the person; structure for the machine.

## Before writing

Pin down: the primary query, the search intent (learn, compare, buy, find), the reader, and the one action the page should drive. If the user gave none, state your assumption in one line and continue.

## Page structure

- **Title tag**: under about 60 characters, primary query near the front, a reason to click.
- **Meta description**: under about 155 characters, says what the reader gets, ends in a soft call to action.
- **H1**: one, matching the intent. **H2 and H3**: the questions a reader actually has, in a logical order.
- **Opening**: answer the query in the first two or three sentences. This is what gets quoted in AI answers and featured snippets.
- **Body**: short paragraphs, concrete examples, numbers with sources, a table or list where a comparison is being made.
- **FAQ**: four to six real questions with self-contained answers of 40 to 60 words.
- **Internal links**: suggest anchor text and target topics; do not invent URLs.

## Rules

- Use the primary query and close variants naturally. Never stuff keywords; if a sentence reads oddly aloud, rewrite it.
- No invented statistics, quotes, awards or customer names. Mark any figure that needs a source with [source needed].
- Do not promise rankings or traffic.
- Original angle beats length. Do not pad to hit a word count.
- Deliver: title tag, meta description, URL slug suggestion, the content in Markdown, then a short list of suggested internal links and image alt texts.
