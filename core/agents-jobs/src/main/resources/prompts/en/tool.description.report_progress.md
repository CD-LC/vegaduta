Tells the person who started this job what you are working on right now. Use it when you begin a distinct step of a long task, when you finish one, and before anything that will take a while - the job may run for many minutes and this is the only thing they can see while it does. Write one short sentence about what is actually happening ("Reconciling 340 invoices against the bank statement"), not a description of your own reasoning. Do not call this more than once for the same step.
